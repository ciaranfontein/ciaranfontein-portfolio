# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring managers, engineering leaders, and recruiters evaluating Ciaran for React Native, mobile product engineering, and closely related frontend roles. They need to understand his level, verify his work quickly, and decide whether to contact him.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio provides evidence that Ciaran can independently build, ship, operate, and improve production mobile products. Success means a qualified employer understands his fit within seconds, sees enough proof to trust the claim, and contacts him or downloads his resume.

## Positioning

Ciaran is a React Native and TypeScript product engineer with six years of production ownership, strong mobile UX judgment, cross-functional delivery experience, and hands-on mobile QA automation. React Native remains the primary identity. Testing experience strengthens the engineering story without repositioning him as a testing specialist, and AI operations remains secondary.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from LinkedIn, a job application, a recruiter message, or a direct referral. They may scan on a phone between candidates or inspect case-study evidence on desktop. The most important actions are viewing shipped products and production work, downloading the resume, and contacting Ciaran.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is an Astro static website and must remain fast, accessible, responsive, and usable without a client-side framework.
- Existing privacy-policy routes and resume downloads must continue working.
- Education dates are omitted from public-facing portfolio and resume content unless specifically requested in an application form.
- Payment testing is limited to Stripe card PaymentSheet staging/test-mode coverage. Do not claim Apple Pay experience or completed production payment certification.
- Cross-role messaging testing was designed and instrumented as a coordinated two-device protocol. Do not call it fully automated.
- Do not invent metrics, customers, employers, credentials, or product capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the professional name Ciaran Fontein.
- Keep the headshot, shipped products, Guusto case study, colleague recommendations, and resume downloads.
- The site should feel personal, technically credible, and product-minded.
- Ciaran is humble and serious. The design should be calm, restrained, and confident enough to let the work speak without theatrical self-promotion.
- Draw cultural inspiration from major Osaka-area technology employers: Panasonic's calm space and real-work imagery, Keyence's factual product-first clarity, and Capcom's confidence in its own product imagery. Do not imitate their branding.
- Avoid generic developer-portfolio styling, an agency identity, an AI-operations-heavy identity, tactile ephemera, decorative stamps, paper textures, brewery-menu styling, or a busy recruitment-site collage.
- The voice should be direct, specific, plainspoken, and free of stock AI-writing patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

- Production Guusto application screenshots, app icon, store links, and six years of ownership details.
- Shipped independent products: KiraKiran, Meigaza, Fate, and the maRuNail website.
- A current headshot.
- LinkedIn recommendations from Felipe Santos da Rocha, Sarah Tong, and Sreeja Guttula.
- A current one-page React Native/frontend resume with Maestro and YAML mobile QA experience.
- Public GitHub and LinkedIn profiles.

## Product Principles

1. Show shipped proof before listing tools.
2. Make production ownership and mobile UX judgment unmistakable.
3. Let specific work and colleague testimony establish seniority.
4. Keep React Native primary while showing useful breadth.
5. Make the next action obvious without sounding like a sales funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, descriptive alternative text, and sufficient contrast in light and dark conditions.
